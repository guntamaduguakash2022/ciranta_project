Built by people who kept switching careers.

Ciranta started as a study group for people mid-career-change. We kept the parts that worked — small cohorts, real mentors, real projects — and cut the rest.

What we believe

Courses fail when they're built to be watched, not used. Every ciranta course is designed backward from a real deliverable — a resume, a dashboard, a campaign plan — so the learning has somewhere to go.

We keep cohorts small on purpose. Mentors who don't know your name can't give you feedback that matters.

How we're different

No infinite video libraries. Every track runs on a fixed start date with a group that moves through it together, which means deadlines, discussion, and people noticing if you fall behind.

Every mentor is currently working the job they teach — not a former practitioner reading old slides.

What we optimize for

Three things guide every course we build.

Finishability

Short, cohort-based tracks people actually complete — not 40-hour libraries that go untouched.

Feedback

Every project gets reviewed by a mentor, not graded by a quiz engine.

Proof of work

You leave with something to show — a portfolio piece, not just a certificate.
